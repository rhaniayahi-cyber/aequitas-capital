03 / Our standards

Our conviction sets
our investment boundaries.

Every holding must pass our exclusion rules. Financial performance cannot justify keeping an ineligible company.

01 — Human rights and international standards
  • Exclude companies flagged for UNGC / OECD violations.
  • Exclude companies flagged for a lack of UNGC / OECD processes.
  • Screen forced labour, child labour and freedom-of-association risks.

The policy records “4/5” for these risk thresholds. The exact comparison operator still needs confirmation before implementation.

02 — Sensitive activities
  • Controversial weapons: exclude when the involvement flag is true.
  • Conventional weapons: exclude above 5% of revenue.
  • Tobacco and adult entertainment: exclude above 0% of revenue.
  • Alcohol: exclude above 5% of revenue.
  • PAB exclusion flag: exclude when the flag is true.
03 — Controversies and data coverage
  • Overall controversy score: exclude at 5/5.
  • Workplace fatalities: exclude above 3 deaths over 3 years.
  • Data coverage: exclude below 50% using the group’s dedicated coverage measure.
  • Screen severe HR and union controversies.

HR and union controversy thresholds are recorded as “4/5”; their exact interpretation remains to be confirmed.

At least 50% of portfolio weight must be invested in companies with positive revenue from the social-impact categories defined in our policy.

This is a portfolio-weight requirement, not a requirement for 50% of each company’s revenue. These are our proposed fund rules. Source: the group’s exclusion policy.

06 / Our engagement

Clear demands.
Accountable follow-up.

Engagement needs a specific request, evidence of progress and a deadline. It cannot override a mandatory exclusion.

MONITOR

Review systematically

Review the investment universe every six months. An overall controversy score of 5 triggers an immediate review.

ACT

Enforce our rules

Excluded holdings receive a zero final weight. The policy sets a maximum of 30 business days to sell following a new normative or sectoral breach during holding.

DOCUMENT

Explain the decision

The ESG analyst applies the rules. The portfolio manager signs off the exclusion list and records the impact of trades.

Our case for the mandate: explicit rules, traceable decisions and a commitment to report what the data can—and cannot—show.

Company-specific engagement targets, deadlines and methodological limitations will be added as the group completes its analysis.
